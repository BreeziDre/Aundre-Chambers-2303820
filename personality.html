LLMs As A Tool, Not A Crutch

In our now higher education, Ai use have changed how students esspecially in computing approach learning

LLMs should be used to enhance the learning process by providing instant explanations, generating practice questions, and offering diverse perspectives on complex topics it should be used has if you had a second brain with alot more IQ. However, relying too much on ai can hinder critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

In conclusion, while LLMs present exciting opportunities for enhancing education, it is important to use them wisely. By treating these models as a extension to ones brain rather than substitutes for independent thought.
